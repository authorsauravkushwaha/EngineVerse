Why students and working engineers use {{ brand.site_name }}

Notes that derive, not describe

Every topic follows one 13-section template: plain explanation, formal definition, intuition, formula with units, full derivation, a worked numerical, real applications, common mistakes, exam questions, interview questions and industry practice.

Practise until it sticks

A fresh Daily Practice set every day, a mistake notebook that brings your weak questions back, and SM-2 spaced repetition so revision happens exactly when you are about to forget.

Run real code, safely

A sandboxed judge executes your submission in an isolated process with no network access, strict resource limits and a hard timeout. Your code never runs inside the web server.
